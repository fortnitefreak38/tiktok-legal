What is Hermes?

Hermes is a small, self-run tool built and used by the owner of the TikTok account "Unsaid Truths". It replaces the manual routine of transferring finished videos to a phone: after a video is produced, Hermes sends it to the owner's own TikTok account, where it arrives as a private post or draft that the owner reviews and publishes in the TikTok app. Hermes is not offered to the public, has no user registration and does not access any other person's account.

Features

Sign in with TikTok

The owner connects their account through TikTok Login Kit. Hermes never sees or stores the TikTok password.

Video upload

Finished vertical videos are sent to the owner's TikTok account through the official Content Posting API.

Private by default

Uploads arrive as private posts or drafts, so nothing goes public until the owner decides in the TikTok app.

Own statistics

Shows the owner's follower count, likes and the list of their own videos with view counts.

How it works

  1. The owner signs in with TikTok and approves the requested permissions on TikTok's own consent screen.
  2. The owner selects a finished video and writes a caption.
  3. Hermes uploads the video to the owner's TikTok account using the Content Posting API.
  4. The owner opens the TikTok app, checks the post, adds a sound if wanted and publishes it.

Permissions and your data

Hermes requests only user.info.basic, user.info.profile, user.info.stats, video.list, video.upload and video.publish, and uses them solely for the features above on the owner's own account. No data is sold or shared with third parties. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings, Security, Manage app permissions.
