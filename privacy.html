Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Controller

Hermes ("the App") is operated by a private individual in Germany. Contact: anormal.buisness@gmail.com

2. Data processed

The App only processes data of its owner's own TikTok account:

  • TikTok OAuth access and refresh tokens
  • Basic profile information (e.g. display name, avatar, open ID)
  • Statistics of the owner's own account and videos (e.g. views, likes, follower count)
  • Videos and captions the owner chooses to publish

No data of other TikTok users is collected, stored, sold or shared.

3. Purpose and legal basis

Data is processed solely to publish the owner's own videos and to display the owner's own statistics (Art. 6(1)(a) and (b) GDPR – consent and performance of the requested service).

4. Storage and processors

OAuth tokens are stored securely by the integration platform Composio, which acts as a technical service provider to connect to the TikTok API. Data is transmitted to TikTok as required to perform the requested actions. No data is used for advertising or sold to third parties.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as the TikTok connection is active. When access is revoked (in TikTok settings or by disconnecting the App), tokens are deleted and no further data is retrieved.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection and to withdraw consent at any time, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact the email above.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
